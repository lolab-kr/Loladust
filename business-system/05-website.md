# 5 — Website (E-commerce Ready)

## 5.1 Platform choice

| Option | Verdict | Why |
|--------|---------|-----|
| **Shopify** | ✅ **Recommended** | Best checkout conversion, native abandoned-cart, PayNow via SG payment apps, product drops via apps, clean Google Shopping feed. Worth the ~$25–50/mo. |
| Wix | ⚠️ OK to start | Cheaper, fine if you're non-technical, but weaker checkout + apps at scale. |
| Instagram + link only | ❌ Not enough | Fine for month 0, but no SEO, no cart recovery, caps you well below $5k. |

**Decision:** launch on **Shopify** (theme: *Dawn* or *Refresh* — minimalist, fast). It pays for
itself the first month you recover 2–3 abandoned carts.

## 5.2 Site structure (conversion-optimised)

```
Home
├─ Shop  ─┬─ Celebration Cakes (6" signature)
│         ├─ Tiramisu (whole + cup boxes)   ← hero SKU, own collection
│         └─ Everyday Bakes (banana loaf, bento)
├─ This Week's Drop        ← scarcity landing page (countdown)
├─ How to Order            ← kills the #1 objection
├─ About                   ← the home-kitchen story
├─ Reviews                 ← social proof
└─ Contact / WhatsApp
Cart → Checkout (PayNow + card) → Thank-you (upsell + follow-up opt-in)
```

## 5.3 Page-by-page layout

### Homepage (top → bottom)
1. **Hero:** full-bleed cake photo + the dusting shot. Headline **"The grown-up celebration
   cake, dusted by hand."** Subhead: "Premium home-baked cakes & Singapore's Gula Melaka
   tiramisu. Islandwide delivery." Primary CTA **Shop the Drop**.
2. **Trust strip:** ★ reviews count · "Made fresh, small-batch" · "Free delivery over $150" · "PayNow".
3. **3 hero products** (cake / tiramisu / bundle) with price + Add to Cart.
4. **This Week's Drop** band with countdown + "X left".
5. **Why Lola Dust** (the 3 USP legs, icon row).
6. **Flavour story** — Gula Melaka mini-section (the moat).
7. **Reviews** carousel (real screenshots).
8. **How to order** (3 steps). 9. **Instagram feed** embed. 10. **Footer:** menu, WhatsApp, delivery info.

### Product page (the money page)
- Large image gallery (hero + cross-section + scale/in-hand + packaging).
- Title, price, **pax served** ("10–12"), lead time, delivery info **above the fold**.
- **Order form:** flavour, message on cake, delivery date (date-picker enforcing lead time),
  add-ons (candles, card, tiramisu cups — the upsell).
- Sticky **Add to Cart**.
- Trust: reviews for *this* product, "small-batch — X made per week".
- **Bundle nudge:** "Complete it — add Gula Melaka tiramisu cups (+$18)".
- FAQ accordion (allergens, halal status, storage, delivery windows).

### About page
The home-kitchen origin story (the soul), your face, your standards ("baked the morning of
delivery"), the flavour philosophy. This page *justifies the premium* — write it like a letter,
not a corporate bio.

### Checkout flow (minimise drop-off)
- Guest checkout on. PayNow + card. Delivery date + slot. Free-delivery threshold shown in cart
  ("$28 to free delivery" — nudges AOV). One-page or accelerated checkout. Post-purchase upsell
  ("add tiramisu cups to your delivery — 1 tap").

singapore`, `premium cake singapore`, `gula melaka tiramisu`, `minimalist cake singapore`,
`bento cake singapore`, `tiramisu delivery singapore`.
