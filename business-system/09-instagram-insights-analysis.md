# 9 — Instagram Insights: Diagnosis & 30-Day Reactivation

*Based on your actual screenshots (Apr–Jul 2026).*

## 9.1 What the numbers say

| Metric | Your data | Reading |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| Followers | **1.1K** | You have a real audience already — this is an asset, not a blank slate. |
| Views, last 90d | **368** | Very low for 1.1K followers. The account is under-distributed. |
| Views, last 30d | **79** · Accounts reached **5** (−50%) | Near-dormant. You're below the 100-reach floor (demographics won't even show). |
| Follower vs non-follower views | **~27% / ~73%** | **Good news:** when you post, the algorithm shows you to *new* people. Discovery works. |
| June | **+484% views, +518% non-follower views** | Proof: the month you posted, reach exploded — then July went quiet and it collapsed. |
| Profile activity | **42 (−36%)**, then **11** | Interest is falling because output fell. Cause and effect. |

**The one-sentence diagnosis:** *You don't have a follower problem or an algorithm problem — you
have a **consistency** problem. The engine works when you feed it; in July you stopped feeding it.*

## 9.2 The two levers (in priority order)

1. **Re-activate the 1.1K you already have.** Most of them haven't seen you in weeks. A *launch
   announcement* (the Founder's Drop) to a warm audience is your fastest first sale — cheaper and
   faster than finding new people.
2. **Feed the discovery engine.** 73% non-follower reach means Reels find strangers *for free*
   when you post consistently. Consistency > perfection.

## 9.3 The 30-day reactivation plan

**Non-negotiable cadence:** **1 Reel/day for the first 14 days**, then settle to 4–5 Reels/week.
This is the single lever that moves everything. (Reach is a function of recent posting velocity.)

### Week 1 — Wake the account up
- Post a Reel **every day**. Use the hooks in `content-swipe-file.md`. Lead with motion in the
  first second (the buttercream swipe / the tiramisu spoon-pull).
- Pin a "we're back, and there's a plan" post. Update bio: *"Premium home-baked cakes 🍰 SG ·
  Gula Melaka tiramisu · Weekly drops → [order link]"*.
- Add Highlights: Menu · Prices · Reviews · How to order · Drops.
- **Goal: reach back above 100/period** (unlocks demographics + signals the algorithm you're active).

### Week 2 — Convert the warm 1.1K
- Announce the **Founder's Drop** (see `06-sales-strategy.md`): waitlist gets 24h early access.
- Daily Stories: countdown sticker, "X of 12 left", behind-the-scenes bake.
- DM your most engaged past commenters personally — they're your first customers.
- **Goal: first sell-out + first 3 reviews (proof).**

### Week 3–4 — Turn discovery into follows + sales
- Settle into the weekly calendar (`03-instagram-strategy.md`): 2 hero Reels + drop cycle.
- Every Reel ends with a reason to follow ("weekly drops — follow so you don't miss Saturday").
- Repost every customer tag (free content + proof). Run the poll → reveal → drop loop.
- **Goal: repeatable weekly rhythm; reach trending up; drop selling out.**

## 9.4 The 3 Instagram KPIs to watch (weekly)
| KPI | Now | 30-day target | 90-day target |
|-----|-----|---------------|---------------|
| Accounts reached / 30d | ~5 | **500+** | 3,000+ |
| Views / 30d | 79 | **1,500+** | 8,000+ |
| Non-follower share | 73% | keep >65% | keep >60% |

If reach isn't climbing after 14 days of daily Reels, the problem is **hook/first-second
retention**, not frequency — swap the opening frames, not the whole strategy.

> The uncomfortable truth: no website or ad spend fixes a quiet feed. The store converts traffic;
> the Reels *are* the traffic. Post first, spend second.
