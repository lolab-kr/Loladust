# 1 — Branding & Naming

## 1.1 The name decision: **Lola Dust**

I checked the "Lola" landscape in-market. It is crowded — which is exactly why the *sub-brand*
matters.

| Candidate | Verdict | Why |
|-----------|---------|-----|
| **Lola Dust** | ✅ **Recommended** | No direct SG cake/bakery match found in search. Distinctive, ownable, memorable. "Dust" evokes icing sugar / cocoa dusting — literal to the craft, and premium-poetic rather than cutesy. |
| Lola Bakes | ❌ Avoid | Generic + crowded. "Lola + Bakes/Bakery/Baked" already used (`lolabaked.com`, `lolabakery.com`, `@lolas_cupcakes`). Hard to rank, hard to own. |
| Lola's / just "Lola" | ❌ Avoid | **Lola's Cafe already operates in Singapore** (Simon Rd). Direct local collision — naming risk. |

**Why "Dust" works as the differentiator:** it's concrete (the finishing dust on a cake), it's
premium without shouting, and it pairs into a tagline system easily — *"Lola Dust — the last
dusting of magic."* It also gives you a visual signature (a soft dusting motif) you can own across
photography, packaging, and the logo.

> **Action before you commit a dollar:** ACRA BizFile name check, IPOS trademark search, then
> secure `@loladust` / `@loladust.sg` on Instagram + TikTok and `loladust.sg` / `.com` domain in
> one sitting. Handles first — they go faster than trademarks.

### Naming architecture
- **Master brand:** Lola Dust
- **Product line names** (gives you "drop" hooks): *The Dust Signature* (celebration cakes),
  *Dust & Cream* (tiramisu line), *Everyday Dust* (banana loaf & bakes).
- **Signature hero SKU:** **Gula Melaka Tiramisu** — the trend-riding flagship (see market research).

## 1.2 Brand identity

| Dimension | Definition |
|-----------|------------|
| **Positioning** | The *grown-up* celebration cake. Premium, home-crafted, design-led — for adults who've outgrown fondant cartoons but don't want a $200+ sculpted showpiece. |
| **Aesthetic** | Minimalist luxe. Buttercream texture (palette-knife, ruffle, painterly), muted/earthy palette, single-tone cakes, gold-leaf and dried-flower accents. Clean negative space in every photo. |
| **Tone of voice** | Warm, quietly confident, a little poetic. Never loud, never discount-y. "Made this morning, in a home kitchen, for your Saturday." |
| **Personality** | If Lola Dust were a person: the friend with impeccable taste who bakes better than the bakery and makes it look effortless. |
| **Values** | Craft over volume · taste first, then beauty · local at heart (SG flavours) · small-batch honesty. |

### Visual identity starter
- **Palette:** warm ivory `#F6F0E6`, cocoa `#4A3428`, dusty rose `#C99B8C`, sage `#9BA986`,
  gold-leaf accent `#C9A227`. (Muted, edible, premium — not neon.)
- **Type:** an elegant high-contrast serif for the logo/headlines (e.g. *Gloock*, *Young Serif*),
  a clean humanist sans for body (e.g. *Instrument Sans*, *Work Sans*). Both are in your installed
  `ui-styling` skill's font set — reuse for the website.
- **Logomark:** wordmark + a soft "dusting" motif (scattered fine dots over the L). Works embossed
  on a plain box — the premium-packaging test.
- **Photography rule:** natural light, one cake, lots of negative space, a dusting caught mid-air
  on the hero shot. Consistency here *is* the brand.

## 1.3 Unique Selling Proposition (USP)

> **"Bakery-grade design, home-kitchen soul, and a Singaporean flavour you can't get at the
> big cake shops — made in small batches, never mass-produced."**

Three legs, each defensible:
1. **Design-led but attainable** — the minimalist buttercream look people screenshot, at
   $150–180 instead of $250+.
2. **Local-flavour signature** — Gula Melaka tiramisu and pandan/kaya twists the chain bakeries
   don't do well. This is the *irreplaceable* hook.
3. **Small-batch scarcity** — limited weekly drops. You literally cannot get it at 4pm on a
   Tuesday, and that's the point.

---

## 1.4 The problem it solves & why it's irreplaceable

**The problem:** In Singapore you can get a *cheap* cake (supermarket, chains) or an *expensive
sculpted showpiece* ($200+). The middle — a genuinely beautiful, genuinely delicious cake for a
grown-up celebration, at a fair premium — is thin. Most "affordable custom" cakes look homemade
in the bad way; most premium cakes are priced for weddings.

**Why Lola Dust is irreplaceable:**
- **Taste + look together.** Competitors win one or the other. Lola Dust refuses to trade off.
- **A flavour identity, not just a design service.** Gula Melaka tiramisu is a *reason to come
  back monthly*, not a once-a-year birthday purchase. That flavour is the moat.
- **Scarcity by design.** Limited drops mean the brand can't be commoditised or undercut — you're
  not competing on "cheapest islandwide delivery," you're the thing people plan their week around.

**The one-line brand promise:** *Lola Dust — the grown-up celebration cake, dusted by hand.*
