## The honest money math (read this first)

You cannot manage to SGD 5,000/month without knowing what 5,000 is *made of*. Here is a
credible mix at your stated prices, and the assumptions are deliberately conservative.

| Product | Price | Orders/mo | Revenue | Role |
|---------|------:|----------:|--------:|------|
| Signature 6" celebration cake (10–12 pax) | $157 | 22 | $3,454 | **Margin engine** — the number everything hangs on |
| Whole tiramisu / tiramisu cup box | ~$52 (est.) | 22 | $1,144 | **Growth engine** — rides the viral trend, drives repeat |
| Banana loaf | ~$17 (est.) | 30 | $510 | **Entry + add-on** — low-friction first purchase, gifting |
| **Total** | | **74 orders** | **$5,108** | Blended AOV ≈ **$69** |

**The one number everything hangs on:** roughly **20–25 signature cakes a month** (~5–6 a
weekend). Miss that and tiramisu volume has to *double* to cover the gap — possible during a
viral spike, not reliable as a baseline. So the whole system is engineered to (a) protect
celebration-cake volume and (b) lift the blended AOV toward **$95** through bundling, which
drops the order count you need from 74 to ~53. **Higher AOV, not more orders, is the scalable lever.**

### Capacity reality
74 orders/month ≈ 2–3 a day, but celebration cakes cluster Fri–Sun. Tiramisu and banana loaf
are batchable and hold better, so they fill weekdays. This is achievable solo **until ~$6–7k**,
at which point decorating hours become the ceiling — that's your first hire (a decorating
assistant), not more ads.

## The strategy in one paragraph (the handoff block)

Lola Dust is a premium, home-based Singapore cake brand positioned as *"the grown-up celebration
cake"* — minimalist, buttercream-textured, quietly luxurious, with a local-flavour signature
(Gula Melaka tiramisu) that gives it a viral top-of-funnel most premium bakers don't have. It
competes below the $200+ sculpted-cake tier (Celebrate With Cake) and above the café/mass tier
(Plain Vanilla, Awfully Chocolate), owning the underserved middle: *design-led but attainable,
$150–180 cakes for adults who want taste and aesthetics over cartoon fondant.* Growth comes from
Reels + limited "drops" that manufacture urgency, converted on a Shopify store built for a
2.5% conversion rate, retained by a lightweight Airtable CRM with WhatsApp follow-ups. First
proof point: one deployed store + one sold-out drop within 30 days.

## Honest limits of this system
- **Name clearance is not legal clearance.** "Lola Dust" looks clear in market search, but before
  you print anything, do a formal check on **ACRA (BizFile)** for the business name and **IPOS**
  for trademark, and grab the `@` handle + `.sg` domain the same day. I can't do the legal search.
- **Competitor exact prices move.** Figures are cited as found (July 2026); re-verify before you
  price against them.
- **Estimated prices** (tiramisu, banana loaf) are placeholders modelled from comparable SG
  bakers — set your real prices from your ingredient cost + target margin (see `06-sales-strategy.md`).
