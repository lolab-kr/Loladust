# 4 — Paid Ads (Instagram + Google)

> **Budget discipline first:** target **CPA ≤ $30** on a first order and **ROAS ≥ 3×**. Start at
> **$20–30/day total** ($600–900/mo). Don't scale a channel until it beats those numbers. Full KPI
> logic in `08-kpis-growth.md`.

## 4.1 Instagram / Meta ads

### Campaign structure
1. **Prospecting — Reels/video** (cold): objective = engagement→traffic, then conversions once
   pixel has data. Creative = your best organic Reels (let winners graduate to paid).
2. **Retargeting** (warm): anyone who viewed a Reel, visited the site, or added to cart.
3. **Lookalike** (scaled): 1–3% lookalike of purchasers + Instagram engagers (needs ~50+ purchases
   first — don't build it in month 1).

### Targeting (Singapore)
- **Location:** Singapore, +delivery radius (whole island — you deliver islandwide).
- **Age/gender:** 24–44, women-weighted (skew from persona; test all-gender for gifting).
- **Interests:** birthday/celebrations, wedding planning, fine dining, cafés (Plain Vanilla,
  Awfully Chocolate as interest proxies), tiramisu/dessert, event planning, expecting/new parents
  (baby showers).
- **Behaviours:** engaged shoppers; recent life-event proxies where available.
- **Retargeting windows:** site visitors 30d, cart-adds 14d, IG engagers 60d.

### Ready-to-launch ad copy (Meta) — 3 angles × primary text / headline / CTA

**Angle A — Aesthetic (celebration cake)**
- *Primary:* "The grown-up birthday cake. Hand-textured buttercream, a whisper of gold, made this
  morning in a home kitchen — not a factory. Islandwide delivery in Singapore. Only a few made
  each week."
- *Headline:* "Cakes for grown-ups · from $157" · *CTA:* Order Now

**Angle B — Flavour/viral (tiramisu — cheapest to acquire)**
- *Primary:* "Everyone's talking about Gula Melaka tiramisu. Ours has a pandan-kaya soul and a
  spoon-pull that stops the scroll. This week's batch is small. 🥄"
- *Headline:* "Singapore's tiramisu, not Italy's" · *CTA:* Get Yours

**Angle C — Scarcity/drop (retargeting)**
- *Primary:* "You looked. It's nearly gone. This Saturday's drop is 12 cakes only — when they're
  gone, they're gone until next week."
- *Headline:* "This week's drop closes Saturday" · *CTA:* Reserve Now

**Hook variations (first line A/B tests):** "Stop buying supermarket cakes." · "Found the baker
before everyone else." · "$157 and it sold out in minutes." · "The cake your dinner guests
screenshot."

## 4.2 Google Ads

### Strategy
High-intent search capture. Two campaign types:
1. **Search** — brand + high-intent non-brand.
2. **Performance Max / Shopping** (once the Shopify feed is live) for "cake" visual queries.

### Keyword set (build ad groups around these)

**High-intent (bid up):**
`customised cake singapore` · `birthday cake delivery singapore` · `premium cake singapore` ·
`order cake online singapore` · `cake delivery islandwide singapore` · `same day cake delivery singapore`

**Flavour / trend (cheap, on-brand):**
`tiramisu singapore` · `gula melaka tiramisu` · `best tiramisu singapore` · `pandan cake singapore`

**Occasion (segment by ad group):**
`hen party cake singapore` · `bridal shower cake` · `anniversary cake delivery singapore` ·
`minimalist birthday cake singapore` · `bento cake singapore`

**Negative keywords (don't pay for these):**
`free` · `recipe` · `how to make` · `jobs` · `halal certification` (unless certified) · `cheap` ·
`wholesale` · `supermarket` · competitor brand names (unless running conquesting deliberately).

## 4.3 Retargeting & lookalikes (the compounding layer)

| Layer | Audience | Message | When to turn on |
|-------|----------|---------|-----------------|
| **Retarget — cart** | Added to cart, no purchase (14d) | "Your cake's still waiting — this week's slots are almost gone" + abandoned-cart flow (see CRM) | Week 1 of ads |
| **Retarget — viewers** | Reel/site viewers (30–60d) | Angle C scarcity | Week 1 |
| **Lookalike 1%** | of purchasers | Best-performing prospecting creative | After ~50 purchases (≈ month 2–3) |
| **Lookalike — engagers** | of IG engagers | Flavour angle (cheap top-funnel) | Month 2 |

**Golden rule:** organic winners become paid ads. Never write a "paid-only" creative before an
organic Reel has proven the hook for free.
