| **Awfully Chocolate** | Mass-premium chain | Islandwide, same/next-day | Chocolate specialist since 1998, convenience | Convenience is their moat, not design. You win on aesthetics + local flavour. |
| **Edith Patisserie / Bob the Baker Boy / Frosted Chick** | Mid custom | Free delivery **>$150**, flat **$15** otherwise; Bob delivery **$11.90** | Custom birthday, broad occasions | The crowded middle. Differentiate hard on *minimalist adult* aesthetic + flavour. |

**Pricing reference points that matter for you:**
- Free-delivery threshold clusters at **$130–150** across the market → set yours at **$150** (nudges AOV up).
- Flat delivery fee norm is **~$11–15**.
- A 5" premium sculpted cake starts at $200 → your **6" at $157 is credible premium** *if* the
  design and flavour justify it. You are priced *just under* the sculpted tier — perfect.

## 2.2 Market trends (what's actually selling in 2026)

**Design**
- **Minimalist chic** — single-colour, smooth or textured buttercream, gold leaf, dried florals.
- **Bento / lunchbox cakes** — clean lines, neat lettering, pastels. High-margin, low-ingredient, *very* Instagrammable. **Add this as a low-price entry SKU.**
- **Buttercream texture is back** — palette-knife/painterly, ruffles, waves. On-trend *and* faster than fondant.

**Flavour**
- Local fusion is the headline: **Pandan Kaya, Gula Melaka, Yuzu**, pistachio, salted caramel.
- **Halal-friendly** demand is strong and growing → decide early (see gap #3).
- **Tiramisu is now a standalone category** with dedicated shops and seasonal rotations.
  **Gula Melaka / Ondeh Ondeh tiramisu is going viral** (Gula Melaka dessert content +85% on
  regional TikTok, 2024→2026). Single-serve cups sold alongside whole cakes.

**Social**
- Reels/TikTok video is the discovery engine; hashtags now behave like **SEO keywords**.
- Personalisation is *expected*, not premium.

## 2.3 Market gaps → your opportunities

| # | Gap in the market | Lola Dust's play |
|---|-------------------|------------------|
| 1 | The "grown-up minimalist" cake at a fair premium is underserved — most custom cakes skew kids/fondant | Own **adult minimalist celebration cakes** as the core identity |
| 2 | Premium bakers under-exploit **local flavour as a brand** (they treat it as one menu item) | Make **Gula Melaka tiramisu the flagship** — a flavour people follow you for |
| 3 | **Halal** premium-minimalist is thin | If your kitchen allows, position as **Muslim-friendly** (no alcohol/pork-derived) — unlocks a large, loyal, under-served segment. Decide day 1. |
| 4 | Most bakers sell *transactions*; few build **repeat rituals** | Weekly **drops** + a tiramisu line = monthly repeat, not yearly birthday |
| 5 | Design-led bakers are often weak on **taste storytelling** | Lead with *both* — "screenshot-worthy AND the best tiramisu you've had" |

## 2.4 Target audience

**Primary persona — "Aesthetic Celebrant" (core revenue)**
- Female, 25–40, works in Singapore CBD or creative/professional fields, moderate–high income.
- Buys for own/friends' birthdays, hen/bridal showers, small dinners, work milestones.
- **Buying triggers:** it looks beautiful on the table + tastes better than the chains + feels
  "found," not mass-ordered.
- Behaviour: discovers on Instagram Reels/TikTok, screenshots, DMs to order, pays via PayNow.
- Objection: "is it worth $157?" → answered by design + flavour + reviews + scarcity.

**Secondary — "Gifting Professional"**
- 28–45, orders for colleagues/clients/family; wants easy, reliable, premium-looking, delivered.
- Higher AOV via bundles + corporate/recurring. Values convenience and a clean checkout.

**Tertiary — "Trend Tiramisu Buyer" (top-of-funnel)**
- 18–35, follows food trends, buys the viral Gula Melaka tiramisu on impulse.
- *Low AOV, high frequency, cheap to acquire* — the on-ramp that later buys a celebration cake.

**Where they are:** Instagram + TikTok (discovery), Google (high-intent "birthday cake delivery
singapore"), WhatsApp (ordering/service).
