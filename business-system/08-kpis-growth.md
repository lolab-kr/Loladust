# 8 — KPIs & 3-Month Growth Plan

## 8.1 The KPIs that matter (with targets + why)

| KPI | Definition | Target | Why this number |
|-----|-----------|--------|-----------------|
| **Monthly revenue** | Total sales | **$5,000+** | The objective |
| **AOV** (Average Order Value) | Revenue ÷ orders | **$95** (from ~$69 baseline) | Every $ of AOV cuts the orders you need. The main scalable lever. |
| **Conversion rate** (site) | Orders ÷ sessions | **2.5%** | Realistic for high-intent premium food ecom; below 1.5% = fix the product page/price clarity |
| **CPA** (Cost per Acquisition) | Ad spend ÷ new customers | **≤ $30** | Must stay well under AOV; at $30 CPA & $95 AOV first-order margin still positive |
| **ROAS** | Revenue from ads ÷ ad spend | **≥ 3×** | Below 3× on a food product usually means you're buying unprofitable orders |
| **Repeat rate** | % customers ordering ≥ twice | **≥ 25% by month 3** | Repeat is free revenue; the tiramisu on-ramp should drive this |
| **CLV** (Customer Lifetime Value) | AOV × avg orders × margin | **≥ $200** | Justifies spending up to ~$30–50 to acquire; the reason retention > acquisition |
| **Drop sell-through** | Units sold ÷ units offered | **100%** (sell out) | If <100%, batch was too big — shrink it, don't discount |

**The load-bearing number again:** ~**20–25 signature cakes/month**. Track it weekly; it's the
canary. Everything else optimises around protecting it.

### The unit economics that make $5k work
- 53 orders × $95 AOV = **$5,035**.
- At 2.5% conversion, that needs **~2,120 site sessions/month** (~70/day) — from organic Reels +
  ~$25/day ads + Google search.
- Ad spend to acquire the paid share (say 25 new/mo × $30 CPA) ≈ **$750/mo** → keep ad cost
  **<20% of revenue**. Organic + repeat must carry the rest (that's why the drop engine matters).

## 8.2 The 3-month roadmap (week by week)

### Month 1 — Foundation · *Win: live store + one sold-out drop*
- **Wk1:** Lock name (ACRA/IPOS check), grab handles + domain. Set palette/logo/photo rule.
  Stand up Airtable CRM (schema in `07-crm.md`).
- **Wk2:** Build Shopify (Dawn theme), 3 products live with SEO copy (`05-website.md`). "Coming
  soon" + waitlist capture. Start daily Stories teasing. **Goal: 100 waitlist.**
- **Wk3:** **Founder's Drop** to waitlist (24h early) → first sell-out + first reviews. Public
  drop opens. 2 Reels live.
- **Wk4:** Second public drop. Turn on automation #1 (cart) + #2 (review request). First KPI review.

### Month 2 — Fuel · *Win: first profitable-ROAS month with paid orders*
- Meta prospecting ($20–30/day) using your best organic Reels; Google Search on high-intent
  keywords. Retargeting live (cart + viewers).
- Launch the **bundle** ("The Full Table") + checkout add-ons → push AOV toward $95.
- Weekly drops running like clockwork; monthly special drop #1 (themed, higher price).
- Automations #3–#4 (welcome, birthday) live. **Goal: ROAS ≥ 3×, AOV ≥ $85.**

### Month 3 — Scale · *Win: a $5k month, ad cost <30% of revenue*
- Build **lookalike audiences** (needs ~50 purchases now). Scale the winning ad sets only.
- Reactivation (#5) + VIP (#6) automations → drive repeat rate to 25%.
- Add an SEO blog post ("best tiramisu singapore", "minimalist birthday cake ideas").
- Test a small corporate/gifting bundle (high AOV). **Goal: $5,000+, repeat ≥ 25%.**

## 8.3 The optimisation loop (run monthly, forever)

1. **Measure** — pull the 8 KPIs into one Airtable dashboard row per month.
2. **Find the bottleneck** — which stage leaks most?
   - Low *reach* → more/better Reels, test hooks.
   - Low *conversion* → fix product page, price clarity, reviews, checkout friction.
   - Low *AOV* → push bundles/add-ons/threshold.
   - Low *repeat* → tighten CRM follow-ups, birthday flow.
3. **Change one thing** — one variable at a time (so you learn what worked).
4. **Re-measure next month** — keep it if it moved the KPI, revert if not.

**Two tripwires to watch:**
- **CPA creeping over $40** → pause scaling, fix creative/targeting before spending more.
- **Drops stop selling out** → demand signal cooling; shrink batches, refresh the hero flavour,
  lean back into organic before buying traffic.
