## 3.6 The Drop Strategy (your scarcity engine)

The core growth mechanic. **A "drop" = a limited batch, announced ahead, sold in a window,
publicly sold out.**

**Weekly cadence:**
- **Mon:** tease + poll the flavour. **Thu:** reveal + countdown. **Sat 10am:** live, N units only
  (start N=10–12 celebration cakes + 20 tiramisu). **Sat/Sun:** sell-through stories → "SOLD OUT."

**Why it works:** manufactures urgency, makes sold-out *public* (social proof), caps your
workload (you never over-commit), and trains customers to buy *now* not "maybe later."

**Monthly special drop** (higher AOV): a themed limited edition (e.g. "CNY Gula Melaka",
"Mother's Day dusted rose") — pre-order, higher price, waitlist. This is your hype + margin peak.

**Rules that keep it premium:**
- Never extend a sold-out drop ("we don't do more — that's the point").
- Always show the sold-out. Scarcity you don't broadcast isn't scarcity.
- Waitlist every drop (that list feeds the CRM and the next launch).
