# 30 Reels — Lola Dust Reactivation Sprint

Ready-to-shoot Reel scripts for the 30-day plan in `09-instagram-insights-analysis.md`.
Reels **1–14** are your daily sprint (wake the account up). **15–22** launch the Founder's Drop.
**23–30** keep the engine running after.

## How to use this
- **Batch-film.** Shoot 5–7 Reels in one bake session — you're already making the cakes.
- **First second is everything.** The on-screen **Hook** must be readable and the motion must start
  instantly. 73% of your reach is strangers; the hook is what stops them.
- **Every Reel ends with a reason to follow or order.** Never a dead end.
- **Audio:** open Instagram → Reels → find a *currently trending* sound in the style noted (trends
  change weekly; the vibe matters more than the exact track). Use "original audio" for ASMR ones.
- **Format:** 9:16, 7–15s unless noted. Keep captions short; put detail in the first comment.
- **Hashtags:** rotate Sets A/B/C from `content-swipe-file.md` — matched to each Reel's topic.
